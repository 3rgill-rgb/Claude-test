About me

Finance meets systems,
clarity in complexity.

I'm Robin, a Chartered Professional Accountant (CPA) with a track record of transforming how organisations manage their finances — from the systems they run on to the processes that keep them moving. I've led Salesforce implementations for private equity-backed businesses, deployed accounting automation tools like FloQast and Tesorio, and driven end-to-end redesigns of critical finance cycles.

Whether it's overhauling Procure-to-Pay, streamlining Lead-to-Cash, or tightening the Record-to-Report cycle, I bring both the accounting rigour of a CPA and the systems thinking of a hands-on implementer. The result: leaner operations, cleaner data, and finance teams that can focus on strategy instead of firefighting.
